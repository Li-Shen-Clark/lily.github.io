I study how trade and market connections shape local economic outcomes. My research combines quantitative spatial models, panel econometrics, and detailed geographic data, with a focus on China and emerging Asia.

## Job market paper

<a id="local-multipliers"></a>
### The Local Multiplier Effects of Trade: A Quantitative Analysis of China

With Kensuke Suzuki, Xiaocong Xu, and Junfu Zhang.

**Research question.** Why do export shocks generate different effects on output and employment across local economies?

**Approach.** We combine evidence on local export shocks with a multi-sector quantitative spatial model of 31 Chinese provinces. The model includes inter-provincial input-output linkages, non-tradable services, and labor mobility, and is calibrated using China's 2017 MRIO table, ICIO data, and Customs micro-data.

**Contribution.** The analysis connects multiplier differences to domestic production networks and the intra-regional manufacturing trade share. Provincial export-GDP multipliers range from 0.30 to 1.02 in the model's counterfactual simulations. These are model-implied output responses; employment responses have different units and are reported separately.

**My role.** Quantitative model development, structural parameter recovery, calibration-data work, and counterfactual analysis.

## Working papers and preprints

<a id="market-integration"></a>
### High-Speed Railway and Market Integration: Evidence from Vegetable Price Dispersion in China

**Research question.** How is passenger connectivity related to spatial price differences in wholesale commodity markets?

**Data and approach.** A panel of 3.99 million city-pair-month observations covers ten vegetables across 117 Chinese cities from 2014 to 2022. Wholesale prices are combined with smartphone-based passenger-mobility measures and rail connectivity information.

**Evidence.** HSR-connected city pairs exhibit 2–5% lower price dispersion in the reported specifications. Passenger-mobility controls and commodity comparisons help examine possible mechanisms. These conditional associations do not, by themselves, establish a causal effect of high-speed rail.

**Related application.** The [Vegetable Price Intelligence project]({{ '/applied/#price-intelligence' | relative_url }}) uses a broader historical price dataset for forecasting and risk analysis. Its forecasting performance answers a different question from this paper's analysis of market integration.

<a id="population-agglomeration"></a>
### Population Agglomeration and Spatial Reallocation in China

**Research question.** How have population concentration and the geographic distribution of settlement changed across China?

**Data and approach.** Annual WorldPop rasters for 2002–2020 are harmonized at approximately 5 km resolution for descriptive spatial analysis.

**Evidence.** The analysis documents increasing concentration and changes in the population-weighted geographic center. The spatial Gini rises from 0.852 to 0.868, and the population-weighted centroid shifts about 17 km southeast. These patterns describe spatial change rather than identify its causes.

## Methods and data

- **Economic models:** quantitative spatial equilibrium, trade and production networks, calibration, structural inversion, and counterfactual simulation.
- **Empirical methods:** panel econometrics, shift-share instruments, event studies, gravity models, and geospatial measurement.
- **Data:** MRIO and ICIO tables, Customs micro-data, wholesale prices, retail scanner panels, passenger mobility, and population rasters.
- **Tools:** Python, Stata, R, MATLAB, SQL, SAS, GeoPandas, and ArcGIS.
